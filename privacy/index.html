Draft only. This policy is a structural draft written for a proposal build. It is not legal advice and must be reviewed and completed by a qualified solicitor with sight of the venture’s actual data flows, sub-processors and company details before publication.

Who we are

Safari Explorers International operates wildlife conservation expeditions for young people at Dinokeng Big 5 Game Reserve, South Africa.

Registered company details and the appointed Information Officer are to be confirmed.

Contact for any privacy matter: expeditions@safariexplorers.international

The law we work under

As a South African operator we are subject to the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA). Because we deal with families in Singapore, the UAE, Australia and potentially the EU and UK, we apply GDPR-equivalent standards throughout rather than maintaining separate regimes: lawful basis, data minimisation, purpose limitation, retention limits, and a clear route to access, correction and erasure.

Where local law in a family’s own jurisdiction grants stronger rights than POPIA, we will honour the stronger position.

What we collect, and why

At enquiry or application: the explorer’s name, age and home country; the parent or guardian’s name, email and phone; contact preference; and anything you choose to write in the notes field. Lawful basis: steps taken at your request prior to entering a contract.

Once a place is confirmed: passport and travel documentation, emergency contacts, insurance details, dietary requirements, and medical information including conditions, allergies and medication. Lawful basis: performance of a contract, and protection of the vital interests of the child.

During the expedition: incident records where an incident occurs, and photographs where consent has been given.

We do not buy contact data, and we do not sell, rent or share your data with third parties for their own marketing. Ever.

Medical information about a minor

This is the most sensitive category of information we hold and it is treated separately from everything else.

Who can see it: access is restricted to the supervising adults who need it to keep your child safe — the Lead, the Safety Officer and the chaperone — plus the founder responsible for programme administration. It is not accessible to guides, lodge staff, drivers or partner operators as a matter of routine.

How it is stored: in access-controlled digital storage, with a printed emergency copy carried securely by the Safety Officer for the duration of the expedition, because a server is no use in a vehicle with no signal.

Where it is disclosed: to treating medical professionals, an ambulance or air-evacuation service, or a receiving hospital, where doing so is necessary to treat your child. And to your insurer where you ask us to support a claim.

What happens to it afterwards: the printed copy is destroyed within seven days of the expedition ending. The digital record is retained only as long as set out below.

Photography and images of children

Photographs of explorers are only taken and used where a parent or guardian has given specific written consent, separately from the booking itself. Consent can be withdrawn at any time and we will remove the images.

Consent for internal use, such as a record shared with the cohort’s families, is asked for separately from consent for public marketing use. Agreeing to one does not agree to the other.

The imagery currently on this site is AI-generated atmospheric photography containing no identifiable people. It is not documentation of any real expedition and is not presented as such.

How long we keep things

Enquiries that do not proceed: deleted within 12 months.

Applications that do not proceed: deleted within 24 months, so that a family reapplying for a later window does not start from scratch.

Booking and financial records: retained for the period South African tax and company law requires.

Medical information: deleted within 12 months of the expedition ending, unless an incident occurred, in which case the incident record and the medical information relevant to it are retained for as long as needed for insurance and legal purposes.

Incident records involving a minor: retained in line with the safeguarding retention period a solicitor advises.

Who processes data on our behalf

We use third-party services for email, form handling, document storage and payment processing. Each is bound to process data only on our instructions.

Your rights

You can ask us for a copy of everything we hold about you or your child, ask us to correct it, ask us to delete it, or object to a particular use. Write to expeditions@safariexplorers.international and we will respond within 30 days.

Deleting information required for the safe running of an expedition your child is booked on may mean they cannot travel. We will tell you plainly if that is the case rather than quietly acting on it.

You can also complain to the Information Regulator of South Africa, or to the data protection authority in your own country.

Cookies and analytics

This proposal build sets no cookies and runs no analytics or tracking of any kind.

If analytics are added to the live site we intend to use a privacy-preserving, cookieless product, and this policy will be updated before that happens.
