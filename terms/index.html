Terms and booking conditions.

Draft for founder review. This document must be reviewed by a qualified solicitor before the site goes live.

Draft only. These conditions are a structural draft written for a proposal build. They are not legal advice and must be reviewed and completed by a qualified solicitor, against the actual lodge, reserve and supplier contracts, before publication.

1. The contract

A contract exists once we have accepted an application in writing and received the deposit. The contract is between Safari Explorers International and the parent or guardian who makes the booking, who accepts these conditions on behalf of everyone named on it.

2. Deposit and payment

A deposit of US$750 per explorer is payable to hold a place and is non-refundable except under clause 5. The balance is payable 60 days before departure. Places are not held before a deposit is received.

If the balance is not received by the due date we may treat the booking as cancelled by you and clause 4 applies.

3. What is and is not included

Included: eight nights’ accommodation, all meals and drinking water, all in-reserve transport, all game drives and programme activities, full-time qualified guiding and supervision, return airport transfers to OR Tambo International, and all programme materials.

Not included: international flights, travel and medical insurance, visa and documentation fees, personal spending, and anything of a personal nature.

4. Cancellation by you

More than 60 days before departure: the deposit is retained and all other sums paid are refunded.

Between 60 and 30 days before departure: the deposit is retained and 50% of the balance is refunded.

Fewer than 30 days before departure: no refund is available, as costs are committed. This is what travel insurance exists for.

Cancellation takes effect on the day we receive it in writing.

5. Minimum numbers, and cancellation by us

The expedition operates with a minimum of four explorers. If that minimum has not been reached 60 days before departure, we will notify you immediately and refund every sum paid, including the deposit, in full.

We may also cancel for reasons genuinely beyond our control, including natural disaster, epidemic, civil unrest, or a reserve or government closure. In that event we will refund all sums paid to us, less any unrecoverable third-party costs, and will document those costs to you.

In neither case are we liable for flights, visas or other arrangements you made independently, which is why insurance is mandatory.

6. Insurance

Travel and medical insurance covering emergency treatment, hospitalisation, medical evacuation and repatriation is a mandatory condition of travel for every explorer. Details must reach us before the balance due date.

An explorer without valid cover will not be permitted to travel and clause 4 will apply.

7. Medical disclosure and fitness to travel

You must disclose all relevant medical conditions, allergies, medication and dietary requirements at application, and tell us of any change before departure.

We may decline a booking, or decline participation in a specific activity, where we reasonably judge that a condition cannot be safely managed in a remote environment. Where we decline a booking on these grounds after a deposit has been paid, and you disclosed the condition fully and accurately, the deposit is refunded.

Non-disclosure that puts an explorer or the group at risk may result in participation being ended without refund.

8. Behaviour

This programme operates in a free-roaming Big Five environment where instructions from guides and the Safety Officer exist to prevent serious injury and are not advisory.

We may end an explorer’s participation where behaviour endangers themselves, another explorer, staff or wildlife. In that event you are responsible for the cost of the explorer’s early return and no refund is due. We would contact you before taking that step other than in an emergency.

9. Changes to the programme

Activities may be reordered, substituted or withdrawn for weather, wildlife movement, reserve access, or safety. The overall length and character of the programme will not change. Wildlife sightings, including of any particular species, are never guaranteed.

10. Liability

This clause is to be drafted by the venture’s solicitor and is deliberately left blank in this draft.

11. Complaints

If something goes wrong during the expedition, tell the Lead or the Safety Officer at the time so it can be put right there and then. If it cannot be resolved, write to us within 28 days of the expedition ending and we will respond within 14 days.
