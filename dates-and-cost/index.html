Exact intake dates are confirmed six to nine months ahead of each expedition, cross-checked against the calendars of the major international schools in all three markets. Applications are reviewed on a rolling basis and every group is capped at eight explorers.

Said plainly

Yes, the December dates run over Christmas.

19 to 27 December covers Christmas Day. We are not going to bury that at the bottom of a terms page.

Those dates exist because they are the one window that sits inside the year-end break in Singapore, the UAE and Australia at the same time, which is what makes a genuinely international cohort possible rather than a group of eight children from one city.

For some families that is ideal: it is the only stretch of the year when a teenager is free for nine consecutive days, and Christmas around a fire in the bush is not a downgrade. For plenty of others it is completely unworkable, and we would rather you told us that now than felt awkward about it later.

If December does not work, the July 2027 window exists precisely for you, and applying now for July costs you nothing and holds nothing against you.

Why the founding cohort pays less

The first cohort is buying from a team with no past expeditions to point to. The founding rate reflects that. In exchange we ask for honest feedback and, if the expedition earns it, permission to tell your family’s story to the ones who come next.

Included in the fee

  • Eight nights’ accommodation at Dinokeng
  • All meals, snacks and drinking water
  • All in-reserve transport, game drives and activities
  • Full-time qualified field guides and supervision
  • Airport transfers to and from OR Tambo
  • Conservation Passport and all programme materials

Not included

  • International flights
  • Travel and medical insurance, arranged by parents or guardians
  • Visa fees where applicable
  • Personal spending money and souvenirs

The one that matters

Minimum numbers guarantee

This expedition runs with a minimum of four explorers. If we have not reached four by 60 days before departure, we will tell you immediately and refund every payment in full, including the deposit. You will never be left holding a flight booking for an expedition that is not happening.

Booking terms

The commercial detail.

Deposit

Holding a place

A place is held only once a deposit of US$750 per explorer is received. The deposit is non-refundable except under the minimum numbers guarantee above, because it is committed to reserve and lodge bookings on your child’s behalf.

Applications are reviewed before a deposit is requested. Applying does not commit you to anything.

Balance

Payment schedule

The balance of the programme fee is due 60 days before departure. For the December 2026 cohort that is 20 October 2026.

Invoices are issued in USD. Bank charges and any currency conversion costs applied by your bank are not included in the fee.

Cancellation

If you need to cancel

More than 60 days before departure: the deposit is retained, everything else already paid is refunded in full.

Between 60 and 30 days: the deposit is retained and 50% of the balance is refundable.

Fewer than 30 days: no refund is available, because the costs are by then committed. This is exactly what travel insurance exists for, and it is why we require it.

Medical

Medical withdrawal

If your child is medically unfit to travel and you provide a medical certificate, the standard brackets above still apply, and your travel insurance is the route to recovering the balance. We will supply whatever documentation your insurer requires, promptly.

If we judge that a disclosed medical condition cannot be safely managed in a remote Big Five environment, we will say so before you pay a deposit rather than after.

Insurance

Insurance requirement

Travel and medical insurance covering emergency treatment, hospitalisation and medical evacuation is mandatory for every explorer. Cover must be in place before the balance payment date and details supplied to us.

A student without valid cover cannot travel. This is not negotiable and it protects your family more than it protects us.

Changes

Changes by us

Activities may be reordered or substituted for weather, wildlife movement, reserve access or safety. The shape and length of the programme will not change.

If we ever have to change something material, you will hear it from us directly and immediately.
