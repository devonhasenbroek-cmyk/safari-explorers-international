Getting there

Flights, unaccompanied minors and entry requirements.

Flights are arranged by families, not by us. This page exists so you are arranging them with the right information, and so nothing is discovered at a check-in desk.

Routes

All three launch markets have a direct service.

Direct matters more than usual here: it removes a transit, a connection risk and a second set of unaccompanied-minor handovers.

Singapore

SIN → JNB

Singapore Airlines operates a direct service between Singapore Changi and OR Tambo International.

The airline operates an Unaccompanied Minor service. Age bands, fees and rules differ by carrier and must be confirmed directly with the airline at the time of booking.

Travelling together

The group flight option.

Where enough explorers are departing from the same city, we will try to coordinate a common flight so the group travels together and arrives together. Several families have found this is the single thing that makes a first long-haul trip feel manageable.

Where that is not possible, a flight-buddy arrangement pairs explorers on the same routing so no one flies alone.

Neither is confirmed for the December 2026 cohort. Both depend entirely on where the eight explorers turn out to be flying from, which we will not know until the cohort is full. We will tell you as soon as we do, and we will not promise it before then.

On the ground

The airport handover.

Explorers are met inside OR Tambo International Airport by a named member of the team carrying identification, and are not left to find anyone.

Where a child is travelling under an airline’s Unaccompanied Minor service, the handover follows that airline’s procedure and the person named on the airline paperwork is the person who collects them.

The transfer to the reserve takes about an hour. You receive an arrival confirmation within an hour of the group reaching the lodge.

Entry requirements for minors

Read this part properly.

South Africa applies additional documentation requirements to travellers under 18, and they are stricter than most countries. Families are turned away at check-in and at the border every year because of this, usually for paperwork that would have taken an afternoon to arrange.

Requirements have historically involved additional proof of parental relationship and, where a child travels without both parents, documented consent from the non-travelling parent or parents. The precise requirements have been revised more than once, differ depending on the child’s passport and family circumstances, and are not something any tour operator’s website should be your source for.

Do not rely on this page, or on any other operator’s page, for the current rules. Verify directly with your nearest South African high commission, embassy or consulate, and with the South African Department of Home Affairs, well before you book flights. Then confirm the same requirements with your airline, because the airline is who will or will not let your child board.

What we will do: once a place is confirmed, we will tell you exactly which documents we have seen other families in your market need, help you assemble the set, provide any letters of invitation or programme confirmation required, and check the pack over before you travel. What we will not do is tell you the rules are one thing when they may have changed.

Start this the week you book. It is the single most common reason a family loses a trip like this, and it is entirely avoidable.
