In full

The detail behind each of those.

Supervision

Small groups. Dedicated supervision.

Each group is limited to a maximum of eight students and is supported by three dedicated adults: a ratio better than one adult to every three explorers.

Every group is accompanied by Michael Daymond, our Lead Safari & Wildlife Expert, a designated Safety Officer, and a female chaperone. That means male and female supervision at all times, on every activity and overnight.

Explorers are never unaccompanied in a free-roaming Big Five environment. Movement between buildings after dark is escorted.

On every expedition

The Safety Officer

Each group is accompanied by a dedicated Safety Officer with direct operating experience inside Dinokeng Game Reserve. This is a distinct role, not a second job handed to a guide.

The Safety Officer manages guest safety, monitors wildlife behaviour, enforces reserve safety protocol and makes sure students follow procedure while moving through a Big Five environment.

They work inside Dinokeng’s wider safety structure, which includes field guides, anti-poaching teams and specialised K9 units, adding a layer of oversight on top of the reserve’s own.

Medical

Medical support and emergency evacuation

Comprehensive medical and emergency procedures are in place for every expedition. In a medical emergency the student is assessed immediately and the appropriate emergency response is activated.

Where hospital treatment is required, evacuation is arranged by road ambulance or, where clinically necessary and available, by aeromedical evacuation. Our preferred higher-level facility is Netcare Montana Hospital, Pretoria, which operates a 24-hour emergency department and trauma services.

Before departure, parents provide full medical information, allergies, medication requirements and emergency contact details. Every student must hold travel and medical insurance covering emergency treatment and medical evacuation.

A detailed Emergency Response Plan covering evacuation procedure, hospital arrangements and the contact chain is reviewed before every programme.

Staffing

Staff screening, safeguarding and insurance

Every adult working directly with students is subject to identity verification, criminal and background screening, and safeguarding checks before taking part in a programme.

The programme operates under public liability and professional indemnity insurance.

Paperwork

Consent and indemnity documentation

Before departure, parents or legal guardians complete all consent, medical, emergency-treatment and indemnity documentation.

That documentation covers permission to take part in planned activities, relevant medical information, emergency contact details, and formal acknowledgement of the inherent risks of wildlife and outdoor adventure activity.

All documentation must be complete and returned before a student can take part.

Health

Medication and allergy protocols

Parents or guardians provide full details of medication, allergies, medical conditions and dietary requirements before departure.

All prescribed medication must arrive clearly labelled and accompanied by written administration instructions. Requirements are reviewed before each programme and communicated to the supervising adults who need them.

Emergency medication such as inhalers or adrenaline auto-injectors stays immediately accessible at all times, including on drives and walks.

Where a student has a significant allergy or medical condition, an individual management plan is agreed in writing before travel.

Accommodation

Accommodation security and rooming

Students are accommodated at a fully fenced and secure property inside Dinokeng Game Reserve.

Rooming is two students per room, each in their own single bed, with private en-suite bathroom facilities. Male and female students are accommodated separately, and rooming is planned in advance for supervision, privacy and safeguarding.

Supervising adults are accommodated within the same lodge environment and remain available throughout the night.

The one exception is the Day 06 sleep-out, which takes place on a managed open platform under continuous adult supervision. Any family who would prefer their child not to take part can opt out without any effect on the rest of the programme.

Communication

Daily updates home

Parents receive a short written check-in every day of the expedition, sent at a predictable time so it can be planned around rather than waited for.

Each update covers what the group did that day, what is planned next, and confirmation that your child is well. Where there is anything you should know, you hear it from us first and directly, not through your child.

Scheduled phone contact between explorers and home is built into the evening routine. Full detail of times and channels is on the For Parents page.

What is on you

International flights to and from OR Tambo, and travel and medical insurance covering emergency treatment and evacuation, are arranged by parents or guardians. We will advise on timing, routing and what the insurance needs to cover, but we do not book either on your behalf.

Full detail is on Getting There.
